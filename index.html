Trading, risk and AI on one screen.

A web trading platform for brokers. Clients trade in the browser, type orders in plain words, see the risk in money before every trade and get a plan, guards and stats that MT4/MT5 do not have. The AI runs on the broker's own servers. Dealers get a desk that finds toxic flow on its own and hedges the broker's exposure automatically. The broker gets the rest too: a real liquidity provider, partners, prop-firm programs, the regulator's rules, its own brand and languages.

The demo, 2 minutes: the trader, then the dealing desk and the broker's business.

Clients see the risk before they click Buy

Stop loss and take profit are set in pips. The order ticket turns them into money at once.

  • Margin needed and the value of one pip
  • Loss at the stop loss in dollars and as a % of equity
  • A clear warning when there is no stop loss
  • One-click trading switch: off, every quick order asks first; on, it trades at once
  • Depth of market ladder: click a price to place a limit or stop order
  • Pending orders, trailing stops, partial close, hedging and netting accounts

Charts traders expect, and more

Professional charts, built into the platform.

  • 1, 2 or 4 charts on screen, each with its own symbol and timeframe
  • MACD, Stochastic, ATR, RSI, Bollinger, SMA, EMA and tick volume
  • Trend lines, horizontal lines, Fibonacci and rectangles that stay in place on every timeframe
  • Price alerts: a notification reaches the phone or computer even when the platform is closed
  • A countdown to the candle close, dark and light theme, resizable panels

Two front-ends, one platform

Built with Go, with the trader screen in two versions: one in plain JavaScript and one in React. Both look and behave the same.

  • Plain JavaScript: light and fast to load
  • React: components, for teams that already work in React
  • The broker picks the one that fits its team

One login, every account

A client signs in once and moves between all of their accounts, live and demo, each in its own currency. No password per account, as in MT4/MT5.

  • Accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, AUD, CAD, USDT, Bitcoin or Ether, converted at the live market rate
  • Money moves between the client's own accounts in one click, at the live rate
  • Share an account with another person at a level: view only, close only or trade, and take it back at any time
  • Two-factor sign-in, passkeys (fingerprint or face), and a list of the devices signed in
  • Sign-in straight from the broker's CRM

A plan for every trade

The ticket does more than send an order. The client says how much to risk, and the platform does the rest.

  • Size from the risk: "0.5% of my equity" with a stop loss becomes the right number of lots
  • Targets that each close a part of the position, and the stop moves to the entry after the first one
  • Guaranteed stop loss: closes at exactly the stop, even when the market jumps; the premium is shown before the order and paid only if the stop is hit
  • "One cancels the other" orders: a buy above and a sell below the market, the first to fill cancels the other
  • A maximum slippage per order, and for every position the price seen, the fill and the slippage

"Why did my position close?"

The question every support desk hears, answered by the platform itself.

  • What closed it: the stop loss, a target, the take profit, a loss limit or a stop-out
  • The level, the price at that moment, and a gap explained in pips
  • The 1-minute chart around the close, with the level marked

Stats that say what to change

Numbers a trader can act on, and the conclusion in plain words.

  • Win rate, profit factor, average win and loss, result per trade
  • Return from trading only, with deposits left out, and the largest drawdown
  • By symbol, by the hour and day of the trade, buy or sell, with or without a stop loss
  • "Your weakest symbol is USDJPY: 24% of all your losing money"

Guards against a bad day

The rules prop firms use, for every account. The broker sets them per group of accounts; the client can only make them stricter.

  • A daily loss limit and a maximum loss: new trades stop when one is reached, and everything can be closed
  • A maximum size open at once
  • A pause after a run of losing trades
  • A smarter stop-out: only the part needed is closed, not whole positions as in MT4/MT5

Copy trading, built in

One account copies another, on the platform itself, with no plug-ins.

  • The size follows the equity of the two accounts, with a risk multiplier and a lot cap
  • Closes, stops and targets follow the leader; every copy passes the follower's own checks
  • A list of strategies to copy, showing results in percent only, never the money or the name behind them
  • A performance fee paid only on new profit

Every account in one view

All the client's accounts together, in the currency they choose.

  • Live and demo money counted apart
  • Equity, open profit and margin level of every account
  • The net exposure per symbol over all live accounts

AI agents, on a leash

Clients connect Claude, ChatGPT or any AI agent to one account, through MCP. MT5 and cTrader open the whole account to the agent; here the client writes a mandate first, and the platform enforces it on every order.

  • Which symbols, how big, how much it may lose in a day, how many orders
  • Bigger orders wait for the client's approval on the phone, with fingerprint or Face ID
  • A kill switch, and an audit log of every request that cannot be changed
  • The broker can cap the lots of every agent, or turn agents off, per account group

The real cost, and the worst case

Before the click: what the order really costs, and what a bad market would do to the whole account.

  • Spread, commission and swap for a night, a week and a month, in the account's currency
  • The break-even price
  • Prices ±1% and ±3%, every position against you, a weekend gap: equity, margin level, stop-out or not

A history nobody can rewrite

Every trade and deposit is chained to the one before with SHA-256, and the server signs the result. The client's browser keeps the signed receipts, and checks the full statement against them at any time.

  • An edited or deleted old trade shows at once
  • Checked on the client's own computer, or by an auditor with a small tool
  • Proof that a B-book broker plays fair

Orders that watch other markets, and the clock

Things that need an Expert Advisor on a VPS elsewhere, as plain options in the ticket.

  • "Buy EURUSD when gold goes above 2370"
  • Time stops: close after 4 hours, or only if not in profit by then
  • A weekly schedule: every Friday at 21:00 close everything, no weekend gap
  • Copy trading with a drawdown pledge: break it, and every copy stops on its own
  • Notifications with buttons: close the biggest loss or approve an order from the lock screen

The dealing desk

What MT4/MT5 Manager leaves to the dealer by hand, done for them.

  • Toxic flow found automatically: a 0–100 score per client, explained in plain words
  • A-book / B-book routing by rules, with the reason for every position, and a shadow book to test new rules
  • Automatic hedging of the broker's exposure, and a news mode
  • Price guard against bad prices
  • Liquidation map, time machine, and questions in plain words
  • Four-eyes approvals and an audit trail for every change

Toxic flow, caught in minutes

In the demo, a simulated arbitrage client trades bitcoin. Within minutes the desk scores it 80 out of 100, marks it toxic and says why, in plain words.

Where the stop-outs sit

For every price in a range: which client stop losses close, which accounts reach stop-out, and what the broker makes or loses. Plus a forecast of who is closed if the price moves 1, 2 or 4%.

Loss limits, watched by the desk

For prop-style and protected accounts: the accounts that hit a limit come first, then the ones closest to it.

  • Rules per group of accounts, applied the moment they are saved
  • How much of each limit every account has used
  • A reset after review, written in the trail

Every AI agent, under the desk's eye

The dealing desk sees every agent connected to a client account, its mandate and its day, and every request it made. One switch turns them all off.

Everything a broker needs to go live

Not only the trading screen: the back office, the liquidity provider, the partners, the regulator's rules and the IT side are part of the platform.

A real liquidity provider, over FIX

The platform connects to cTrader liquidity (Pepperstone, IC Markets and other cTrader brokers) over FIX 4.4, with its own FIX engine written for this platform.

  • The LP's prices replace the simulated ones, symbol by symbol; the symbol list is read from the LP and matched by name
  • A-book trades and B-book hedges are sent to the LP as market orders, and closed against the right LP position
  • Every order shows the LP's price and the slippage against the desk's price
  • If the LP goes quiet, prices stop: they are never replaced by made-up ones

Partners who bring clients

Introducing brokers (IB) get a sign-up link. Every client who signs up with it is theirs, and every lot that client closes pays them.

  • Rebate per lot for each asset class: forex, gold, indices, crypto…
  • Two levels: the partner, and the partner above it
  • Paid automatically when a live trade closes, into the partner's own account
  • The partner sees the link, the clients and the earnings on the platform itself

A prop firm, out of the box

Funded-trader programs: the trader gets an account of e.g. 100,000 USD and has to reach a target without breaking the loss rules.

  • Phases: Challenge, Verification, Funded with a profit split
  • Daily and maximum loss checked on every price; a broken rule fails the challenge at once
  • A phase passes on its own when the target is reached and the minimum days are done
  • The trader sees the progress above the positions; the desk sees every challenge live

Clients worth a call today

The platform reads its own data and tells the sales team who to call, and why.

  • Ready for a live account: demo clients who trade regularly and carefully, with stop losses, and have not funded an account yet
  • May be leaving: clients who stopped coming, trade much less, withdrew most of their money or were stopped out, with what to do for each
  • Both lists go to the CRM as a file
  • A morning report in plain words reaches the dealers' phones every day at 06:30

A coach before the mistake

Before an order that looks like a known mistake, a short word in plain language. It never blocks the order.

  • Ten times the usual size after five losses in a row
  • Too many orders in a few minutes
  • The same symbol again, a minute after a loss on it
  • No stop loss, or more than 5% of the account at risk

Every trade, played back

Any closed trade plays back on the chart, candle by candle: the entry, the stop loss, the take profit and the close. For learning, and for settling a dispute in a minute.

From a stop-out to a deposit, in one tap

When the margin falls, the warning says what brings the account back, and a button opens the broker's payment page with the amount filled in. It works with the PSP Hub or any payment page.

Your brand, their language

The platform carries the broker's name, logo and colours. Each trader picks a language; Arabic turns the whole screen right to left.

  • English, Greek, Spanish and Arabic today; new languages are one table to translate
  • Set from the back office, with no developer involved
  • The dealing desk stays in English for the broker's staff

News, before it hits

An economic calendar inside the platform: interest rates, jobs reports, inflation.

  • The broker enters announcements or imports them from a file
  • Traders see what is coming for their currencies, and can have positions closed minutes before high-impact news
  • The desk switches its news mode on by itself around high-impact announcements, and tells the dealers

The regulator's rules, built in

For brokers under CySEC, the FCA or any ESMA regulator.

  • Retail leverage limits per asset class, with the ESMA preset in one click
  • Negative balance protection for retail clients; professional clients on their own terms
  • The risk warning on every page, with the real share of losing accounts calculated by the platform
  • Transaction report for regulatory reporting, as a file
  • Market hours, holidays and dividends per symbol; closed markets refuse new orders

Ready for the IT team

What keeps a platform with real money running, day and night.

  • A standby server that copies every change live and can take over in about a minute
  • Automatic backups, kept and downloadable from the back office
  • Monitoring numbers for Prometheus and Grafana
  • A load test tool: about 1,650 orders and closes per second on one test server, every order written to disk
  • Clients and their history move over from MT4/MT5 with their login numbers
