Trading, risk and AI on one screen.

A web trading platform for brokers. Clients trade in the browser, type orders in plain words and see the risk in money before every trade. The AI runs on the broker's own servers.

The full demo, 2.5 minutes: a trader, then the dealing desk (captions in Greek). Prices come from a built-in simulator.

Clients see the risk before they click Buy

Stop loss and take profit are set in pips. The order ticket turns them into money at once.

  • Margin needed and the value of one pip
  • Loss at the stop loss in dollars and as a % of equity
  • A clear warning when there is no stop loss
  • One-click trading from Market watch, plus Close profitable and Close all
  • Pending orders, trailing stops, partial close, hedging and netting accounts

✦An assistant that never trades on its own

buy 0.5 gold, stop 150 pips, tp 300
Shows Buy 0.50 XAUUSD, SL 2335.63, TP 2380.63 and waits for Confirm.
κλείσε τα μισά από το χρυσό
Greek for "close half of the gold": proposes closing 0.25 of 0.50 lots of the gold position.
when price crosses above sma 20 and rsi above 50 on eurusd m5 then buy 0.2 sl 15 tp 30
Becomes a rule the client can test on recent history and then activate. It replaces Expert Advisors, with no code.
τι θα γίνει αν πέσει ο χρυσός 2%;
"What if gold falls 2%?": shows the new equity, the stop losses that would close positions on the way, and the margin level.

The model is open source and runs on the broker's own hardware, so client data never leaves the broker. Every number comes from the trading engine, and the AI only understands the words. Common commands are understood by built-in rules in microseconds, even with the model offline.

The dealing desk, live

Dealers get their own screen with every client.

  • The broker's exposure per symbol, net long or short, with its value
  • Clients closest to stop-out, with margin call flags
  • The latest trades of all clients, stop-outs highlighted
  • Withdrawals with four-eyes approval
  • One-click check of every balance against the ledger

Built for real money

Nothing is lostEvery change is on disk before it is confirmed. After a restart or a power cut the platform comes back exactly as it was.
FastOpening and closing a position takes well under a millisecond, including the safe write to disk.

Exact moneyBalances are counted in whole cents and checked against an append-only ledger.
Reports without slowing tradingA PostgreSQL copy for back office and CRM is filled in the background.
